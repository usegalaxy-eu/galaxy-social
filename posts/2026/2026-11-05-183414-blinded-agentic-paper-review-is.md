Blinded agentic paper review is coming soon to usegalaxy.org, bringing fast manuscript checks to the research community at scale.

In a new preprint from Temple University, the Translational Genomics Research Institute, the University of Cape Town, and The Pennsylvania State University, we built BARS (Blinded AI Review System) and applied it across 1,874 blind reviews of 1,714 biomedical papers. After stripping titles, authors, institutions, journals, and dates, BARS evaluates methods, evidence alignment, biology, and reproducibility. Across our 200-paper calibration set, BARS passed 100% of verified sound controls (8.6/10 mean), removed a +0.35-point prestige boost on everyday pilot papers, and more than doubled human referee consistency across repeated blind runs of a 30-paper test-retest subset—in under three minutes for four cents per paper.

This study demonstrates the power of agentic review of papers. For the research community, BARS supports three practical workflows:
• Pre-submission checks to verify table math, controls, and statistical reporting.
• Full-draft verification for collaborators joining multi-lab studies.
• Mentored training where students compare critiques against a calibrated blind review.
