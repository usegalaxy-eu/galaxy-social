Poster Session II at CSHL Biological Data Science 2026 is tonight (Friday, 5:00-7:00pm), with two more from our group:

Orbit: Reproducible Agentic Workflow Development with Cloud Execution Included (Poster 126, live demo)
Anton Nekrutenko, Scott Cain and Dannon Baker aren't just showing a poster here — they'll be running Orbit live. Orbit is a desktop app for sketching, running and publishing complete analyses, tightly integrated with Galaxy and NCBI Datasets, able to run workflows across the Texas Advanced Computing Center, the Jetstream2 cloud and the ACCESS-CI consortium, and provider-agnostic across commercial and free frontier models. Stop by to watch it turn a plain-language request into an approved plan, a prototype run on a data subset via Galaxy User-Defined Tools, and a full cloud run — producing a shareable Galaxy history, workflow or notebook, not a folder of local scripts only one person can rerun.
No poster PDF for this one — it's a live demo, not a printed poster.

disassembler: planetary-scale sequence extraction from Logan (Poster 159)
Andrew Seacord and colleagues built two complementary tools, a graph path-walker and a streaming contig assembler, to pull target sequences directly out of Logan's petabase-scale SRA index without a reference-alignment pipeline. Applied to SARS-CoV-2 spike alone, they scanned 4.99 million Logan runs, drove a contamination filter from 0.555% mislabeled accessions down to zero, and ran a selection scan across 161 global haplotypes in under 30 seconds — flagging the known antibody-escape site E484K. The same approach also turned up obelisks across microbiome datasets and built a reference panel for H. pylori.
Poster PDF: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nekrut/cshl-meetings/main/2026/biological-data-science/seacord/poster.pdf
