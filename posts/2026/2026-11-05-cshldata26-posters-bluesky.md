Six posters from the Galaxy Project team are up this week at CSHL Biological Data Science 2026 (Nov 4–7): AI-agent reanalysis, Galaxy user-defined tools, workflow automation, cannabis genomics, genome assembly, and SRA mining at planetary scale. Follow #CSHLdata26.


HighGen (Poster 52): a 218-assembly cannabis genomics resource (Burhans & Nekrutenko, Penn State) with a UCSC track hub and real MULTIZ/masking-bias fixes. https://github.com/nekrut/cshl-meetings/blob/main/2026/biological-data-science/burhans/cannabis-poster.pdf #CSHLdata26


Orbit + Galaxy User-Defined Tools (Poster 54): an AI agent reproducing 5 published genomics studies from raw reads as 67+ versioned, containerized tools with full provenance. https://github.com/nekrut/cshl-meetings/blob/main/2026/biological-data-science/cain/orbit-poster.pdf #CSHLdata26


Galaxy Workflow Foundry (Poster 62): a reviewable knowledge base compiled into agent skills, turning a paper into a tested Galaxy workflow. DengueSeq run: 7/7 steps validated. https://github.com/nekrut/cshl-meetings/blob/main/2026/biological-data-science/chilton/poster.pdf #CSHLdata26


VGP + Galaxy (Poster 105): dual-haplotype curation across 817 vertebrate genomes raises chromosome assignment from 95% to 97% vs. single curation. https://github.com/nekrut/cshl-meetings/blob/main/2026/biological-data-science/dlariviere/poster.pdf #CSHLdata26


disassembler (Poster 159): pulling target sequences straight out of Logan's petabase SRA index. SARS-CoV-2 spike: 4.99M runs scanned, contamination cut to 0%. https://github.com/nekrut/cshl-meetings/blob/main/2026/biological-data-science/seacord/poster.pdf #CSHLdata26


Galaxy user-defined tools (Poster 61): interface, environment, data, compute, provenance and sharing in ~40 lines, in your own account, no admin install. https://github.com/nekrut/cshl-meetings/blob/main/2026/biological-data-science/vandenbeek/udt-poster.pdf #CSHLdata26


Orbit: Reproducible Agentic Workflow Development with Cloud Execution Included (Poster 126, live demo). Nekrutenko, Cain & Baker demo Orbit live: a desktop app that plans, runs and publishes analyses on Galaxy + NCBI Datasets, across TACC/Jetstream2/ACCESS-CI. #CSHLdata26
