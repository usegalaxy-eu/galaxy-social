The miaDash Shiny app from the @bioconductor@genomic.social microbiome team is now an interactive tool on usegalaxy.eu: import, analyse and visualise microbiome data without writing a line of code.
https://galaxyproject.org/news/2026-10-21-miadash-interactive-tool/
![The miaDash logo](https://galaxyproject.org/images/news/2026-10-21-miadash-interactive-tool/static/mia_logo.png)

Output panels include abundance barplots, heatmaps, ordination, scree and loading plots, trees, networks, scatter plots and boxplots, alongside data exports to Galaxy.](https://galaxyproject.org/images/news/2026-10-21-miadash-interactive-tool/static/pipeline.png)
